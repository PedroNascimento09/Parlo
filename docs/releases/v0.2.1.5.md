Parlo 0.2.1.5 answers while it works. A transcription no longer freezes every other request the local server receives, the model list says **In use** while the recogniser is busy, and Home shows the three most recent results under the status.

## Highlights

- Every connection gets its own thread, so a health check or the model list answers while a transcription runs.
- The model list marks the recogniser **In use** while it works, from the last snapshot the engine gave, without asking the engine anything.
- The engine still transcribes one request at a time, and the release says so instead of hiding it.
- Home lists the three most recent dictations with their time, their language pair and the program they landed in.
- The chosen languages, the shortcut and the engine now sit behind their own locks, with a test that fails if the session ever stops being safe to share between threads.

## Measured

- Tests: **125** in `parlo-cli` and **79** in `parlo-core`, with `clippy -D warnings`, `cargo fmt --check` and `node --check` clean.
- `POST /dictate` with ten seconds of silence through the real engine: **2.47 s**, reported latency **2438 ms**.
- `GET /models` answered **in use** at the first poll, 0.2 s into that request, and **loaded** once it finished.
- With another request in flight, `/health` answered **200 in 3.0 ms** and `/app.css` **200 in 3.6 ms**.

## Install

Download `Parlo-Setup.exe` from this release and run it. From 0.2.1.2 onward the update arrives inside the app with these notes beside the button; on 0.2.1 or 0.2.1.1 run the installer once, because the page in those versions cannot start an update.

## Known limitations

- Only the recogniser is marked **In use**. The translation pair is not, because whether a request translated depends on the language it wrote into, and the app does not guess.
- The end-to-end check used silence, not a spoken sentence. No real speaker has been recorded through this path yet.
- The engine transcribes one request at a time, so two dictations sent together wait for each other.
- Models already on disk from 0.2.1.2 or earlier keep the Brazilian voice until the European one is downloaded and the old one removed.
- The installer is not signed, and macOS and Linux are not supported.
